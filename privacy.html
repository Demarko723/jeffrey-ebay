Privacy policy: Jeffrey eBay (personal app)

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This is a private, single-user app. It is used only by its owner to see their own eBay seller account on their own home computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own active listings, offers, buyer questions and recent sales. It has no feature to change listings, prices or offers, or to send messages.

What it keeps

  • Only the owner's own eBay sign-in tokens, stored on the owner's device and readable only by the owner.
  • eBay data is kept in memory for up to 10 minutes and is never written to disk.
  • Buyer names, addresses, emails and phone numbers are removed when the data arrives and are never stored, shown or sent anywhere. Only a buyer's eBay username may be shown to the owner.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. There are no cookies, analytics or trackers on these pages.
